## Decisions

1. **`webGLMemorySize` stays 512.** No measured OOM / growth evidence yet;
   even the s3tc-present closure estimate (415.9 MB worst case, all skybox
   sets loaded at once) fits under the 512 MB ceiling only if N13's skybox
   caps land and unused skybox sets are not all live simultaneously.
   Raising above 512 requires a **measured** OOM or heap > 400 MB from the
   block above — per the card guard, no raise without measured need.
2. **If s3tc is absent on the Chromebook** (common on ARM Mali/Adreno —
   treat as *inferred risk*, not fact until the env line says otherwise),
   the RGBA32 closure (1756 MB) is unreachable. **N13 becomes mandatory**
   (2048/DXT1/Crunch caps on all skybox faces) and, on top of that,
   per-texture `maxTextureSize` must be reduced so the RGBA32 sum is
   < 150 MB; the concrete reduction list is produced by
   `python3 tools/texture_audit.py --scene Assets/Scenes/main.unity --markdown`
   (start with the 2048×2048 DXT5 entries above: 1024 cuts them 4×).
3. **If s3tc is present**, keep current sizes; the s3tc-present closure
   (415.9 MB) still exceeds 150 MB only because of the 48 `Resources/Skybox`
   assets — after N13 caps and dropping unused skybox sets, expect < 150 MB.
4. No readable (`isReadable: 1`) textures exist in the project, so there is
   no CPU-side RGBA32 doubling.
5. **Not changed by this card:** `webGLMemorySize` (512),
   `webGLLinkerTarget` (1, WebGL2 only), `webGLThreadsSupport` (0).
